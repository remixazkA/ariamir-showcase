# Hardware Sponsors and Validation Partners

Ariamir welcomes technically relevant hardware evaluation and sponsorship discussions.

## Areas of interest

- GPUs and AI accelerators
- FPGAs and heterogeneous compute
- CPUs and workstation platforms
- RAM and high-capacity memory configurations
- NVMe / storage
- cooling and thermal solutions
- power supplies and chassis
- networking
- capture, camera and media hardware
- workstation peripherals relevant to AI development and testing

## What Ariamir can provide

Depending on the collaboration:

- documented installation/compatibility testing;
- workload-oriented performance observations;
- sanitized screenshots or demos;
- inclusion in the Hardware Compatibility Lab;
- reproducible benchmark methodology where practical;
- engineering feedback on suitability for local AI workflows;
- public acknowledgement when both sides approve it.

## Independence and disclosure

Receiving evaluation hardware does not guarantee endorsement or a favourable benchmark result. Public materials should distinguish sponsored/provided hardware from independently purchased hardware when that distinction is relevant.

A company is not described as an Ariamir sponsor or partner in this repository until the relationship is approved for public acknowledgement.

## Technical fit example: FPGA

Ariamir's public FPGA evaluation thesis is to treat the device as a heterogeneous resource for workloads such as deterministic preprocessing, streaming, specialised kernels and low-latency pipelines, coordinated alongside CPU/GPU resources.

The orchestration implementation and routing logic are proprietary and are not disclosed by this showcase.
